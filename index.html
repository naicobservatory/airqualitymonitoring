Common Sources

PM10 Sources

  • → Dust and soil particles
  • → Construction and demolition
  • → Road dust and abrasion
  • → Pollen and sea salt
  • → Volcanic ash

PM2.5 Sources

  • → Vehicle exhaust and emissions
  • → Industrial pollution
  • → Power plant emissions
  • → Burning of fuel
  • → Chemical reactions in air

How They Affect Your Health

PM10 Health Effects

Deposition

Deposits in upper airway and throat

Symptoms

Coughing, wheezing, throat irritation

Risk Level

Moderate — primarily respiratory

PM2.5 Health Effects

Deposition

Penetrates deep into lungs and bloodstream

Symptoms

Asthma, bronchitis, cardiovascular issues

Risk Level

High — systemic and respiratory effects

Understanding AQI Levels

How Overall AQI is Calculated: The Air Quality Index is provided directly by the environmental monitoring gateway. The highest individual sub-index (between PM2.5 and PM10) determines the overall reported AQI.
